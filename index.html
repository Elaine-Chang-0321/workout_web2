Cardio Exercise

Cardio exercise refers to any activity that gets your heart rate up and makes you breathe faster, such as running, swimming, or cycling. It helps improve cardiovascular function, burn fat, and boost mood.

Strength Training

Strength training involves increasing muscle strength and endurance through resistance, such as weightlifting, squats, and push-ups. It helps increase muscle mass, boost metabolism, and prevent osteoporosis.
